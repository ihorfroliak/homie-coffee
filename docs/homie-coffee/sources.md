# sources.md — every external claim used in Gate 0

Generated from the research records, not hand-typed, so a citation cannot drift from the claim it
supports. Each row: **key · claim · value · tag · source · publication date · confidence**.
All accessed 2026-08-20/21.

**Tags.** `fact` = read directly off the cited page. `estimate` = derived, averaged, or described
by the source itself as indicative. `inference` = reasoned conclusion, not stated by any source —
these carry no URL by design and must never be cited as external evidence.

**Standing caveat.** Prices, tariffs, fee schedules and legal deadlines change. Every figure is
as-read on its access date. Anything used to commit money or to satisfy a legal obligation must be
re-checked at the time of the decision, not trusted from this file.

**Where a page could not be read** — 403, 404, TLS failure, paywall — that is recorded in the gaps
section of the relevant document rather than substituted from memory. Absence of a number here
means it was not obtainable, never that it was assumed.
